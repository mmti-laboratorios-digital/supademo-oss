### Categorização de features
- **Core:** Boards, Demos (unlimited), Forms, Capture editing, Custom themes, Checklists, MCP server, **Sandbox demos**, Custom domains, AI voiceovers, HTML capture translations.
- **Copilot (AI):** Generate, custom context, prompt & review, upload, media edits, capture editing, custom use cases.
- **AI Avatars:** avatar cloning, AI avatar minutes (100/300/300).
- **Integrations:** Slack, HubSpot, Email, Webhook, Salesforce, Marketo.
- **Launchpad:** Sales sequences (Gong, Salesloft, Outreach, Gmail, Outlook, Seismic, Highspot).
- **Playbooks:** Interest level demos, Requests/approvals, Personal intro videos, Rep contact card, **Blacklisted domains**, Demo signals.
- **Analytics:** Basic, Advanced filters, Interest areas, A/B testing, Buyer circle discovery, Pipeline impact.
- **Security:** SSO, Directory sync, Custom permissions, Audit logs, Blacklisted domains, **Multiple teams/workspace captures**.
