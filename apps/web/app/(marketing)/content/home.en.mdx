## 3 ways to start in minutes

### 1. Capture — Chrome extension, 1 click

Install the extension, open your SaaS, click record. DemoBR takes screenshots, captures clicks and hotspots — no heavy HTML cloning, no ghost server running. Each step becomes a clickable point in the player.

### 2. Play — Portuguese-first player

The player loads fast, walks step by step, plays TTS narration if you want, has an LGPD banner by default, a lead form when configured, and a native WhatsApp CTA. Fully responsive, embeddable on any page.

### 3. Share — public link, embed, or QR Code

Each demo becomes a public link `demobr.com.br/d/your-slug`. Works as an embed (iframe), an open link, or a QR Code you can print at events. Anonymous analytics by default, opt-in with 1 click.
